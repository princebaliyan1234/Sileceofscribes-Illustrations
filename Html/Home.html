What authors say

Questions, answered

01

How does the design process work?

Once you place an order, I start by reviewing your book details, genre, and references. Based on that, I create a rough sketch cover concept tailored to your story. After that once the basic idea is finished I move to color and depth for the cover and at last comes the title.

02

How long will my cover take?

Basic: 10 days, Standard: 14, Premium: 21. Days 1-2; I review your story and gather references. Then I send linework for your feedback, add base colors, then depth, highlights and background, and finish with the title. Need it sooner? Add Fast Delivery (7, 10 or 14 days).

03

What information do I need to send before work starts?

To begin smoothly, please share: book title and author name; genre and short summary of the story; trim size and page count (for print covers); any reference covers or style preferences (optional). The more details you provide, the better the final result will match your vision.

04

What if I don’t like the result? How do revisions work?

Revisions are included based on the package you choose. You can request changes to colors, typography, layout, or overall feel so the cover aligns better with your expectations. Revisions are meant to refine the approved concept, not to create an entirely new design from scratch.

05

Can I send example covers or references?

Absolutely. You’re welcome to share example covers, illustrations, or styles you like. References help me understand your expectations better and ensure the final design matches your vision and genre.

06

What are source files, revisions, file formats, and 3D mockups?

Source files are editable design files included in specific packages, allowing future edits. Revisions are the number of changes you can request. Final files are high-resolution, print-ready formats. 3D mockups are realistic previews for display or promotion.

07

Can you match a specific art style?

Yes. If you have reference images or examples, I'll use them as inspiration while creating a cover suited to your story.

08

Will my cover fit Web-Novel, Royal Road or Tapas?

Yes. Every cover is delivered in platform-ready dimensions suitable for digital publishing.

09

What if I don't have a clear idea?

That's completely fine. Tell me about your story, and I'll help develop a cover concept that matches its tone and genre, once we have it we can move forward and make that into an actual good cover

10

Do you design series branding?

Yes. I can keep typography, colors, and overall visual style consistent across multiple volumes.
